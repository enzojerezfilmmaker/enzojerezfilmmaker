Consulting

Make your videos reflect the true quality of your project, through a filmmaker’s eyes.

Your project, business, or organization has something valuable to say. Yet often, the videos you share on social media don't convey it with the clarity, depth, or visual quality it deserves.

I help you define what story to tell, how to shoot it with strong visual and sound standards, and which pieces to create, so your social media presence genuinely connects with the right people—without losing your essence.
Who it is for
01

Entrepreneurs and independent professionals

Therapists, coaches and professionals who want to communicate their work with clarity and coherence.

02

Artists, writers and musicians

Creators who want more visibility and a digital presence that is true to their work.

03

Associations and event organizers

Cultural centers, associations and projects that need to reach more people with a clear message.

04

Small businesses

Businesses that want a more polished image that is truly their own.

What we work on
01

Your story on camera

We define what you want to share in your videos and how to structure your message to capture attention from the very first seconds, without sounding scripted.

02

Visual and sound aesthetics

I teach you how to elevate the visual and audio quality of your recordings—framing, basic lighting, clean sound, and pacing—using the gear you already have.

03

Planning your video pieces

We decide which video formats your project actually needs for social media and how to organize your shoots without stress.

04

Curating your video presence

We review your current videos and profiles to bring visual coherence to how you present yourself—so you stop posting just for the sake of posting.
