---
title: "Mentoring"
---

I offer mentoring for people who want to develop their ability to create, communicate, and collaborate through audiovisual work.

**1. On-Camera Expression**  
Mentoring for people who want to express themselves naturally and effectively in front of the camera, through facial expression, body language, and physical presence.

**2. Creative Collaboration**  
Mentoring for independent audiovisual technicians who want to build creative teams of artists and technicians capable of working together in a synergistic, effective, and mutually supportive way.

**3. Artistic Self-Production**  
Mentoring for artists who want to produce audiovisual work independently, for themselves and through their own creative vision, while achieving both technical quality and artistic coherence.

**4. Entering Audiovisual Storytelling**  
Mentoring for people without an established artistic background who want to explore the creation of audiovisual stories and discover their own creative possibilities through filmmaking.
