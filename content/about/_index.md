title: "About"
---

I am drawn to auteur cinema and to the pursuit of narrative excellence and original storytelling. I believe in the power of a strong visual language, precise sound, and an organic approach to filmmaking—one in which every element of the audiovisual process contributes to the life of the work.

For me, filmmaking can be a transformative and almost thaumaturgical experience: a process capable of changing those who take part in its creation, and, ideally, of leaving a lasting impression on the viewer as well.
