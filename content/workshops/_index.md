---
title: "Workshops"
---

I offer auteur filmmaking workshops for adults who want to learn how to create audiovisual work to professional technical standards.

The workshops are designed for people who may not have a technical background in filmmaking or an established artistic career, but who have a desire to tell stories through the moving image.

Through a practical and creative approach, participants explore the process of developing and producing short fiction films and documentaries, from the first idea to the finished work.
